# What is missing, how much we have, how to get it, and how to invent it last

Each line: what the game wants; what we actually have; how to get the real thing; the last-resort rule for inventing
it from the partial data. "Get" items marked (reachable) can be fetched from this environment; others need you.

## Boats and harbors

- **Mooring-by-mooring sizes (the flier you saw).** Have: counts per cove, Avalon's row rule by boat length,
  the 100 ft rigging ceiling and 60 ft scarcity at Two Harbors, state records of min and max lengths, the mooring
  tackle spec. Missing: which buoy is which size. Get: the Two Harbors Policy and Procedures Manual (April 2024)
  has per-cove mooring maps as images in Appendix C pages 42 to 52, and Avalon's 2018-19 brochure has a row chart;
  both are public PDFs (reachable, need a visual read). Invent: assign sizes by row, smallest inshore, using
  Avalon's row rule for every cove.
- **Boats present on the day.** Have: hull detections with lengths on 11 May 2022 aerial imagery for calm water
  (Boats tab), monthly occupancy statistics (Avalon ~170 boats a night in October), fleet lists. Missing: the
  October mix per cove, boats anchored off. Get: Sentinel-2 10 m images from any October (reachable) show hulls
  over about 15 m; the Two Harbors harbor department could give October counts. Invent: draw N boats per cove
  from the October occupancy (Avalon 170, Isthmus Cove 60, Cat Harbor 25, Cherry and Fourth of July 20, Emerald
  and Howlands 10, others 0 to 5 on a weekday) with lengths sampled from the detected distribution.
- **Boat condition after the wave.** Have: run-up bands and the state hazard line; Cat Harbor's record of showing
  every tsunami. Missing: any hydrodynamics. Get: a GeoClaw run on NOAA CUDEM bathymetry (reachable data, a
  day's work). Invent: boats on moorings inside the 10.5 m band are lost; Cat Harbor halves the run-up; anchored
  boats in over 100 ft of water ride it out.

## Shelter

- **Bunkers.** Have: two WWII gun-emplacement bunkers between Cottonwood Cove and Ben Weston Beach (trail
  documented), Camp Cactus with five standing buildings on the windward side behind Mt Orizaba, Coast Guard fuel
  tanks under the Isthmus barracks area, concrete tanks under the Toyon basketball court. Missing: coordinates
  for the Ben Weston bunkers and Camp Cactus, interior condition, access. Get: the Hiking Project trail page
  gives the spur geometry (reachable); the USACE formerly-used-defense-sites report for Camp Cactus gives a
  location. Invent: place Camp Cactus 1 km SW of Cactus Peak in the gully, the bunkers on the 60 m knoll north of
  Ben Weston Beach; both dry, unlit, half-collapsed.
- **Mines.** Have: USGS coordinates and workings for Black Jack, Renton and Cherry Valley; reporting that most are
  sealed and flooded. Missing: which adits are open. Get: nothing public; the Conservancy knows. Invent: Black
  Jack's upper adit open for 100 ft then flooded; Renton's lowest crosscut open 120 m; both usable as fallout
  shelter with radon-style air problems after days.
- **Caves.** Have: named sea caves and headland caves, Torqua Cave. Missing: a list. Invent: 10 to 20 shallow
  caves on the Isthmus headlands, none deeper than 15 m, all within the run-up band.
- **Hardened buildings.** Have: the Casino (concrete), hospital, plant, tanks, schools, camps; OSM gives
  building type for about a third of the 1,910 footprints. Missing: construction and basements for the rest.
  Invent: concrete for Casino, plant, tanks, hospital, school, airport hangar; wood frame everywhere else.
- **The Casino as a shelter.** Have: a 2002 book's claim of two weeks of food and water for the year-round
  population, medium confidence. Get: the City of Avalon's Local Hazard Mitigation Plan is being written now
  (ask the fire chief). Invent: keep the claim; stock for 4,000 people for 14 days.

## Water, food, fuel

- **Tank volumes.** Have: 13 SCE tanks mapped as 26 storage tanks and 10 covered reservoirs in OSM; the three
  desal tanks total 375,000 gal; the Million Gallon Tank name. Missing: each tank's volume and what it holds.
  Get: SCE's Catalina water fact sheet and CPUC filings list tank sizes (reachable PDFs). Invent: volume from
  footprint times 6 m height; tanks above towns hold water, tanks at Pebbly Beach hold fuel.
- **Wells and whether Two Harbors has its own.** Have: 10 wells, Avalon's three in Middle Canyon, the pipeline to
  the west end. Missing: well locations. Invent: wells along Middle Canyon and at Cottonwood; Two Harbors depends
  on the pipeline and the Million Gallon Tank (about 3 days of village use).
- **Food stock.** Have: Vons (06:00 to 22:00), the Two Harbors General Store (groceries, camp fuel, pizza),
  over 20 restaurants, the barge Monday to Friday, 4 to 5 grocery trailers up to three times a week. Missing:
  inventory. Invent: Vons holds 3 to 5 days for the resident population, the General Store 1 to 2 days for Two
  Harbors, restaurants a day each; cruise ships carry weeks.
- **Fuel stock outside SCE.** Have: SCE's tanks and reserve, the fuel docks' grades. Missing: the Avalon fuel
  dock's four underground tanks and the gas stations' tanks. Invent: 4 by 10,000 gal at the fuel dock, 2 by
  10,000 at Pebbly Beach, 2,000 gal at Two Harbors.

## Fallout and weather

- **Where the plume goes.** Have: the prevailing onshore wind (plume inland), Santa Ana reversal in autumn, the
  7:10 decay rule. Missing: any island-specific deposition. Get: NOAA HYSPLIT trajectories for the date
  (reachable, free); the NWS hourly forecast we already pull. Invent: roll the wind at the flash; a Santa Ana day
  puts the island in the plume for 6 to 12 h, otherwise it is clean.
- **Radiation levels.** Nothing island-specific exists. Invent: dose rate from the 7:10 rule scaled by the wind
  roll; shelter factors: underground 50, concrete building 10, wood house 3, tent 1.

## People and traffic

- **Who is where at noon.** Have: monthly arrival totals, cruise schedule by day, residents, hotel and rental
  occupancy, campground capacities. Missing: day-of-week split, per-campground October occupancy, the Trans-Catalina
  Trail permit count. Get: the tourism authority's monthly report series (reachable) for more months; the
  Conservancy for permit counts. Invent: weekday factor 0.7 on daily averages; campgrounds 30 to 50% full on an
  October weekday; 10 to 30 hikers on the trail.
- **Two Harbors population.** Have: 150 to 300. Invent: 200.
- **Hotel rooms.** Have: six properties' counts and 330 short-term rentals. Invent: 1,000 rooms in Avalon.

## Vehicles

- Have: category counts and the 800 to 860 full-size figure for Avalon, transit and tour fleets, fire apparatus
  from fan rosters. Missing: Two Harbors vehicles, Conservancy and SCE fleet sizes, the Sheriff's boats, quarry
  equipment today. Invent: Two Harbors 15 pickups and 10 carts; Conservancy 25 trucks; SCE 15; quarry 2 loaders,
  2 haul trucks, 1 drill rig; Middle Ranch 5 trucks and a backhoe.

## Map data

- **Vegetation.** Have: OSM wood polygons (few). Get: LANDFIRE existing vegetation type and canopy cover
  (reachable) or the Conservancy's vegetation map (ArcGIS Online). Invent: coastal sage on south slopes, chaparral
  on north slopes, oak and ironwood in canyons above 300 m, grassland on the plateau.
- **Near-shore bathymetry.** Have: ETOPO through the Terrarium tiles (coarse). Get: NOAA CUDEM 1/9 arc-second
  topobathy tiles for Catalina (reachable).
- **Trails.** Have: 465 OSM trail and track segments, 151 miles, the Trans-Catalina Trail relation. Missing:
  closures and conditions. Get: the Conservancy trail map.
- **OSM holes found while building the atlas:** the Two Harbors fuel dock, Two Harbors gas station and fire
  station 155 are not mapped; many Avalon businesses lack building outlines; there are no bunker, adit or cave
  tags on the island; mooring fields are not mapped as areas.

## Animals

- **Bison on the map.** Have: herd under 90, central-island range from 2001-02 data, the airport basin and
  Escondido road as the usual places, campground visits. Missing: a current GPS or sighting layer. Get: the
  Conservancy's rangers track them; iNaturalist sightings (reachable API) give recent points. Invent: three
  groups of 15 to 30 in the airport basin, Cape Canyon and the Escondido grassland, plus 5 to 10 lone bulls
  anywhere east of the Isthmus; none on the West End.
- **Deer on the map.** Have: 2,040 interior at 27 per square mile with Middle Canyon the densest transect, 150 in
  Avalon, removal in progress in Middle Ranch this October. Invent: density by habitat (gardens and canyons
  high, bare ridges low); 10% fewer each month of the removal.
- **Fox, cats, rats.** Have: fox 2,162 at 10 per square km; cats 600 to 750 (old); rats everywhere. Invent:
  uniform fox density, cats around the towns and camps, rats in every building.
- **Eagles and raptors.** Have: 8 nest sites named. Missing: exact coordinates (webcams exist for West End and Two
  Harbors). Invent: nests at the named landmarks.
- **Sea lions.** Have: "hundreds" at Seal Rocks, haul-outs at Black Point. Invent: 200 at Seal Rocks.
- **Kelp.** Have: thinned since 2014, healthier on the West End and backside. Get: Sentinel-2 kelp canopy
  (reachable) or the Santa Barbara LTER kelp canopy dataset. Invent: kelp beds on every rocky point.
