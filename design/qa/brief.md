> There is a repo now called "escape from Catalina Island" Also, I have an idea for turning this into a game. Escape from Catalina. I have other ideas for a backpacker game but my idea is that a nuke hits La and the San Pedro shelf or whatever collapses causing a tsunami. You have arrived in two Harbors and see it happen. It's too far away for any real direct effects from the blast, but the first thing that happens is the tsunami. It wipes out Avalon into Harbors. I don't know whether it will affect anything on the opposite side of the island to the same degree. The player would have to effectively find higher ground after gathering as much resources as they can within the time constraints of seeing the wave approach from LA 25-26 miles away. Once that is through the next problem, would ultimately then be the fallout. There are some storage facilities that go into the side of the mountain I believe, but I could be wrong. I know there's some science stations and such on the island. We would then need to avoid fallout I think. Although I don't really understand how long that would last, so it would then probably turn into a weight to wait indoors with your resources, survival rationing type thing and trying to intelligently determine how long to wait. Wait too long and your supplies run out or maybe other things become problematic for the next stage somehow. The next goal would effectively then be to try to find a way to get back to the mainland or somewhere close by that wasn't affected by the blast so that you can get help from SOS teams on the mainland.
